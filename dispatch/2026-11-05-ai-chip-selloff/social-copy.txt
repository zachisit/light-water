Chips broke first. Everything else followed.

On Thursday the semiconductor index fell 12.2% and the Nasdaq 5.5%, its biggest drop in 19 months. The selling started in Asian chipmakers overnight and reached every U.S. sector after the open. Micron and AMD fell about 17%; the cloud giants that buy their chips fell 2% to 4%.

What didn't happen matters too: Treasuries rose, the dollar was flat and high-yield bonds dipped just 0.3%. That looks like a repricing of AI chip stocks, not a financial crisis.
