COMPANY ACCOUNT - Light Water Capital
========================================================================
Light Water Capital is pleased to announce that Shardul Mehetar has been named Lead Quantitative Research Intern within our Systematic Strategies group, effective October 1.

Since joining the firm in June, Shardul has:

- Co-authored two published strategy studies: a live-built RSI-divergence strategy on the Nasdaq 100, and a 60-backtest study of a 50-EMA New York session breakout across EURUSD, GBPUSD and XAUUSD on 16.5 years of hourly data. Both went through Monte Carlo, walk-forward and data-integrity validation.
- Extended July Backtester, our open-source research engine, with point-in-time universe screens that eliminate survivorship bias, magnitude-weighted price-jump validation, and tooling to verify published outputs.
- Authored a seven-part empirical study of core market behaviors across U.S. equities back to 2004: momentum, volatility clustering, shock response, volume, resiliency, commonality and correlation. Each was tested against its own null hypothesis, and 155 persistence tests were corrected for overlapping windows. The study also found variance far more predictable than direction, which supports volatility targeting.

The designation recognizes the breadth and rigor of this work. Congratulations, Shardul.
