1. COMPANY ACCOUNT - Light Water Capital
========================================================================
The most expensive bug in a backtester is the one that makes a strategy look better than it is.

So before this release, we went looking for those bugs. We ran an adversarial review of July Backtester, our open-source research engine, and every finding had to come with a reproduction we could run. There were 17, and the five rated High all leaned the same way: in the strategy's favor.

- A stop the market gapped through at 80 still filled at 95.
- Under open execution, stops weren't checked on the fill bar, so a trade that hit its stop on day one could be booked as a winner.
- Walk-forward measured the wrong window, so a real out-of-sample collapse from 10% to 2% could pass.
- In testing, all 200 of 200 zero-edge strategies passed the Monte Carlo performance check.
- Equity shorts reserved no collateral, so a 10%-per-position portfolio could end up 300% gross short.

v1.16 fixes all 17. Sizing now runs through one code path, point-in-time universes resolve to securities rather than reused tickers, and Monte Carlo runs can be seeded so they repeat.

Some backtests will look worse after upgrading. That is the point. Re-run before you compare with older results; every changed default can be switched back with one setting.

Thanks to Shardul Mehetar, Angad Bhatti and Suriya Narayanan Rajavel on our Systematic Strategies team, who shipped real parts of this release. Angad's first contributions are in it.

July Backtester is MIT-licensed and runs free on Yahoo Finance data with no API key. Full write-up in the first comment.
