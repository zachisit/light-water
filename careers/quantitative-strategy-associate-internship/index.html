Light Water Capital

Quant Research & Strategy Intern

Audit the equity strategies we already run. Then build new ones, in multiple asset classes, and take them to the live tape.

  • Location Remote
  • Commitment 20 hours a week
  • Term 3 months, option to extend
  • Compensation Unpaid mentorship internship
  • Team Systematic Strategies

The role

Light Water Capital is a private investment firm and family office in Corydon, Indiana. We manage our own capital across several asset classes, and we run those strategies ourselves.

We are looking for one person to join the existing intern cohort: to extend the research already underway and to lead the development of new strategies. You would work directly with the founder and with the other interns, refining the common stock strategies we run live today and building the next set from the ground floor.

The work

  • Equity strategy audit

    Review the common stock models we run live to find performance gaps and mathematical drift, then help modify the existing logic so it holds in the current market regime.

  • Strategy development

    Own new strategies end to end — research, foundational modeling, building, testing, iterating. From the thought to the execution, they are yours.

  • Backtesting and data

    Use our own backtesting infrastructure to validate models against slippage, liquidity constraints and asset-specific fees — including July Backtester, the Python engine we built in-house and open-sourced, with Monte Carlo, walk-forward analysis and regime detection. Data comes from paid vendors and in-house sourcing, so the tooling is not the obstacle.

  • Live deployment

    This is not a theoretical exercise. Models that hold up are prepared for deployment against real capital.

  • Real ownership

    Genuine responsibility from the first week: finding where the alpha leaked out of prior research, proving your own ideas in public, and working alongside a capable cohort.

Who you are

  • Mathematically rigorous

    You are comfortable with expectancy, variance and R-multiple distributions, and you know why the distribution matters more than the average.

  • Technically proficient

    Strong with Python or a comparable data language. Experience with Claude or Codex, and an active subscription to one of them at the $20 a month tier or above.

  • Market-agnostic

    You treat an edge as a mathematical property, and you expect it to translate across equities, FX and crypto.

  • High agency

    Given a high-level thesis, you can build the proof of concept independently, without being handed a manual.

What you get

  • Direct access

    You work with the founder on strategy logic and on the execution architecture behind it, not at two removes from either.

  • Ground-floor ownership

    You are not maintaining a legacy system. You are helping build the one that replaces it.

  • Research that reaches the tape

    You see your own work move from a backtest to live capital, and you see what the market does with it.

  • Public recognition

    We acknowledge our interns publicly on our LinkedIn and write about the work they produce, and you can publish under your own byline in The Dispatch — something that stays useful long after the term ends.

Compensation and compliance

This is an unpaid internship with a for-profit firm, structured as an educational experience in compliance with LinkedIn policy and U.S. Department of Labor guidance under the Fair Labor Standards Act. The program is designed primarily for the benefit of the intern, does not displace paid employees, and provides structured training and mentorship. There is no expectation of wages or of guaranteed employment.
