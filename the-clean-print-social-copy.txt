Core CPI cooled to 2.5% in July. That's the right direction — but it isn't a new inflation regime.

The July report gave markets a genuinely clean core print: +0.2% on the month, 2.5% over the year — the lowest reading of 2026. And shelter, which drove so much of the spring's stickiness, finally stepped back (+0.1%), doing far less of the heavy lifting.

But "better" isn't "finished." Core landed on consensus, not below it. Energy is still +14.7% higher than a year ago even after a soft month. And a single on-consensus print lowers the odds the Fed has to react to July — it doesn't establish a trend.
